## App content

1. **Privacy policy:** `https://www.funny1.oursharedcode.com/privacy`. The app links to it from
   Home (Step 85).
2. **App access:** all functionality is available without special access (no login).
3. **Ads:** No, the app contains no ads.
4. **Content rating** (the IARC questionnaire), category *Game*. The evidence for the content
   answers is `docs/PICTURE_REVIEW.md`: 1,245 library pictures read, 10,000 machine-scored, ten
   blocked in Funny One, and the built-in art judged fit for a Families audience as drawn.
   - **Violence:** No. Knights and pirates carry costume props; no fight, injury or blood is
     shown. If you read a knight pointing a toy sword as "cartoon or fantasy violence", Yes is
     also honest; it may raise the rating a step in some regions.
   - **Fear / horror:** No. The scariest answer is a toaster "with glowing eyes", played for
     laughs.
   - **Sexuality or nudity:** No. **Language:** No — every answer is pre-written; the picture
     text is gibberish. **Crude humour:** No.
   - **Alcohol, tobacco, drugs:** No. **Gambling or simulated gambling:** No.
   - **Users interact or exchange content:** No — one device passed around; no online play, no
     chat. Sharing a finished story uses the phone's own share sheet, at the player's choice.
   - **Shares the user's location:** No. **Digital purchases:** No. **Unrestricted internet
     access:** No — the app opens only the game; its two outside links open in the browser.
5. **Target audience and content:** ages 6–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17 and 18 and over (owner decision
   2026-10-19); not 5 and under. Children are in it, so the Families policy applies:
   - content suitable for children — the picture review above;
   - no ads and no data collected — items 3 and 8;
   - a privacy policy in the listing and inside the app — item 1;
   - "must not merely provide a webview of a website … without permission from the website owner
     or administrator" (Families policy, read 2026-10-19). We own and administer the site, and the
     app proves it to Android through Digital Asset Links; it is a Trusted Web Activity (Chrome),
     not a WebView. If a reviewer still rejects it on this point, reply with that.
6. **News app:** No. **Health, financial features, government app:** none.
7. **AI-generated content:** out of scope. Play's AI-Generated Content policy covers apps that
   create content with AI from user prompts; "apps that merely host AI-generated content and are
   unable to create content using AI" are out of scope (Play Console Help, answers 13985936 and
   14094294, read 2026-10-19). Funny One only downloads pictures drawn in advance, by address.
8. **Data safety:** "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?" → **No.**
   - Names stay in the device's memory; the language and picture choice stay on the device.
     Data that never leaves the device is not "collected" in Play's sense.
   - "From the Internet" downloads a picture by its address only, made from the game's
     pre-written phrases. Cloudflare sees the IP address in its technical logs, as any web host
     does; we receive nothing, and an IP address alone is not one of Play's data types.
   - The game's own server keeps no access logs (the privacy policy, Service providers).
   - The form then skips its remaining questions, and the listing shows "No data collected".
